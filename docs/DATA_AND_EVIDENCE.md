# Public demo data and evidence policy

**Status:** Proposed; required before any real case-study material is added.

## Public demonstrations

The public application must use synthetic businesses, fictional employees, synthetic qualifications/assessments and fictional incidents. Scenarios may be inspired by general management problems encountered in the author's career, but should not reconstruct private employment records with superficial name changes.

## Four distinct content categories

| Category | Meaning | Publication requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Historical experience | Events, responsibilities and outcomes that actually occurred | Confirm accuracy, attribution, confidentiality and permission |
| Reconstructed practice | General methods recalled and recreated after the fact | Mark as reconstruction, not an original employer record |
| Modern redesign | Practices proposed now based on experience and new research | Explain that they are proposals, not historically implemented |
| Synthetic scenario | Entirely fictional entities, incidents, figures and outcomes | Clearly label fiction and expose assumptions |

## Private information never to commit

Employee names, assessments, complaints, health/disability records, personnel files, salary data tied to identifiable individuals, unreleased contracts, customer lists, reference contacts, commercially confidential materials, access keys, personal identifiers or permission correspondence.

Anonymity requires more than removing a name. If a case could identify a person or business through role, dates and circumstances, revise or exclude it.

## Metrics and decision outputs

Use explicit denominators, comparison periods, evidence strength and uncertainty. Keep approved contract value distinct from recognised revenue; distinguish project gross margin from business EBITDA; do not imply that this later demonstration software caused earlier operational results.

A manager retains responsibility for consequential decisions. The simulation should show options and trade-offs, not issue automatic promotion, dismissal or discipline.

## Research use

Official standards, professional research and vendors may inform the design; record publication date, jurisdiction, relevance, licensing constraints and opposing evidence. Do not claim ISO certification, legal compliance or independent endorsement without appropriate verification.

## Pre-publication check

Before merging any historical content: verify that the statement is supportable; confirm its source classification; check permission and confidentiality; review unintended identifiability; verify that illustrations are visibly fictional; record any uncertainty.
