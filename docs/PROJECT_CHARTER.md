# Project charter

**Project:** Workforce Capability & Development  
**Status:** Proposal for owner review; not yet formally approved  
**Accountable owner and sole maintainer:** NathanTaylorOps  
**Current phase:** Project establishment and discovery

## Objective

Demonstrate how a competent operations/general-management leader develops people, builds other leaders, creates fair and accountable workplaces and improves organisational capability.

The goal is a *credible demonstration of management judgment*, not a generic HR administration platform. The project should complement existing public portfolio work in commercial decisions, investment analysis, project controls, resource readiness and supply resilience.

## Intended audiences

1. Australian owner, CEO, GM/COO hiring panel and executive recruiter — needs clear evidence of real management experience and judgment.
2. Managers, supervisors and employees — need understandable, practical and humane scenarios.
3. HR/People & Culture — needs transparent, support-oriented and fair practices, with context-specific escalation.
4. Technical reviewers — need coherent domain rules, reproducible scenarios, high-quality UX and tested implementation.

## Product proposition

**Work requirement → role expectations → evidence of competence → learning and coaching → bounded authority → considered leadership action → review and learning.**

### Connected areas to investigate

- Organisation and role accountability
- Competence and practical training
- Coaching, mentoring and development
- Delegation and leadership
- Fair people management and culture
- Succession and workforce strategy
- Executive decisions and review
- Guided scenarios and credible supporting evidence

These are investigation areas, not a commitment to eight separate application modules.

## Flagship experiences

1. Develop a future leader in a fictional custom-construction business.
2. Handle a difficult people, quality or culture decision with employee voice and managerial accountability.
3. Improve organisational structure and succession in a fully fictional larger organisation.

A technical manufacturing example and a commercial estimating example may support these scenarios.

## Important boundaries

- **All public interactive employee, company and performance data must be synthetic.**
- Genuine professional experience must be separated from invented demonstrations; verified case studies require confidentiality, attribution and publication review.
- An employee's training completion is not equivalent to competence, a current licence, permission or available capacity.
- Employees must retain meaningful agency; manager support and work-design responsibilities must be explicit.
- No automated dismissal, promotion, disciplinary decisions, psychometric ranking or individual-worth scores.
- No real HR records, applicant-tracking, payroll, timesheets, production tenant management or claims of regulatory certification.
- Results must be explainable and assumptions visible; simulated benefits are not historical business outcomes.

## Success criteria

- A recruiter can quickly identify the *real* leadership experience the project illustrates and tell it apart from its fiction.
- A manager can complete a substantial leadership decision journey and understand the options, evidence, constraints and consequences.
- An employee or HR reviewer can identify employee voice, support, accountability and suitable escalation.
- A technical reviewer can trace critical decisions to documented rules, test cases and reproducible demo states.
- The public project is accessible, practical, attractive and demonstrably tested.

These are intended validation criteria, **not achieved results**.

## Project authority

The maintainer decides scope, architecture, acceptance and release readiness. Independent feedback is sought and attributed honestly when it actually occurs. A self-review is not described as an independent review.

## Approval needed before implementation

Confirm the principal hiring audience, historical-evidence publication boundaries, flagship workflow, priority features, technical architecture and first-release cut list. Until then, application development is not authorised by this charter.
