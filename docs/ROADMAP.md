# Discovery and delivery roadmap

**Status:** Proposed; milestone completion must be evidenced in subsequent issues/PRs.

## D0 — Project foundation (current)

Agree project name, purpose, audience, public-data boundary, working process and a manageable discovery backlog.

**Exit condition:** Owner accepts the charter and documentation; no sensitive material is present in the public repository.

## D1 — Research and real leadership evidence

Reconstruct one real, anonymised account of developing an employee or future manager, plus an estimator-coaching method and a practical manufacturing training process. Separately investigate relevant Australian hiring expectations, management/HR guidance and competing workflows.

**Exit condition:** Source and claim classifications are explicit; gaps remain visible; no private evidence is published prematurely.

## D2 — Leadership domain and scenario discovery

Define employees' and managers' perspectives, role requirements, assessments, qualified assessors, authority, coaching support, learning transfer, difficult conversations, cultural accountability, succession and alternatives. Storyboard the flagship with both successes and setbacks.

**Exit condition:** The primary journey has coherent state transitions, realistic alternatives, safeguards and initial acceptance examples.

## D3 — UX and engineering exploration

Create and test wireframes for an employer-facing overview, guided decision journey and detailed demo. Define canonical data, versioned synthetic fixtures, decision logic, reproducibility and accessibility. Compare appropriate technical architectures and record the decision.

**Exit condition:** A prototype can be evaluated with realistic tasks; critical rules are traceable to planned tests.

## D4 — Authorise a focused first release

Finalise initial product requirements, scope exclusions, data rules, threat assessment, UX direction, build backlog and test plan.

**Exit condition:** Owner approves P0 functionality and the delivery approach.

## B0 — Technical foundation

Prepare project structure, automated checks, types/schema validation and initial design system.

## B1 — First complete leadership journey

Deliver one employer-ready, synthetic employee-to-leader development experience with interactive decisions, manager follow-through, a setback, explained outcomes and reproducible state.

## B2 — People and culture

Deliver a properly researched difficult-decision pathway and focused training/quality examples.

## B3 — Organisational strategy

Deliver management depth, succession and workforce investment in a fictional larger organisation.

## B4 — Release validation

Complete accessible UX, regression tests, realistic reviewer tasks, provenance-checked case studies, honest screenshots, performance verification and public release notes.

## Prioritisation

- **P0:** Essential for the first complete, credible leadership journey and public-data safety.
- **P1:** High-value follow-on functionality that does not block B1.
- **P2:** Optional expansion only if evidence justifies it.

This roadmap is deliberately not a delivery-date promise. Tasks will be promoted to issues only when requirements and acceptance criteria are clear. New features must justify what they improve and what they defer.
